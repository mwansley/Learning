START HERE — YOUR AI RELATIONSHIP NOTEBOOK

Everything you need is in this folder.
Take a little look ahead before we meet. Preparation is optional.

1. OPEN YOUR NOTEBOOK
Open Relationship-Notebook.html in a web browser on your computer. Prefer paper or a simple phone preview? Open the Print folder, then Client-Preview-Notebook.pdf.

2. LOOK AHEAD, AT YOUR OWN PACE
Choose Open my notebook. Read the nine questions, jot down thoughts, or leave them blank. Skip, combine options, or use your own words.

3. SAVE BEFORE YOU CLOSE
If you type notes, choose Pause & save, then Download my notebook. Keep that file to reopen later through Pause & save > Open a saved notebook. Notes do not save automatically.

4. BRING IT TO OUR SESSION
Bring only the notes or handwritten pages you want to share. Together, we will use Start in my AI chat > Copy for my AI chat, paste it into the agreed AI chat, and review the settings it proposes.

KEEP PRIVATE
Leave private answers out of the files or photos you share. In the HTML notebook, Keep private clears that answer and excludes it from future saves and handoffs. It cannot remove copies already downloaded or uploaded.

The folders hold optional printouts and reference materials. Start with the HTML notebook; explore the folders only if needed.
